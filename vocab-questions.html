JLPT vocabulary questions — fill in the blank and similar meaning

Free, no-login practice for two kinds of vocabulary question on the Japanese-Language Proficiency Test, at N5, N4 and N3. In fill in the blank (文脈規定) you choose the word that fits a sentence, out of four words of the same kind and level — nouns, verbs in the form the sentence needs (集めて, 起きます), い- and な-adjectives. In similar meaning (言い換え類義) you choose the sentence that means about the same as the underlined part: おととい and ふつかまえ, 留守です and 家にいません, 延期になった and 後の日に変わった. Every answer comes with the meaning of all four words or a short note, and the English of the sentence. Ten questions at a time, with optional furigana. Each question is also a flashcard: mark it Again or Good, and the due ones come first. The same questions are also printable as PDF worksheets with the answers on the last page.
