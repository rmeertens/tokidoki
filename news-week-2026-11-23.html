Easy Japanese news: 23 November – 29 November 2026

15 stories from Japan, each told in simple Japanese at JLPT N5, N4 and N3.

  • A record 359,424 elementary and junior high school students stayed away from school last year, the 13th rise in a row (学校を　長く　休む　子どもが　いちばん　多く　なりました)
  • The government plans to name several "backup capitals" next autumn, ready to take over if a big disaster hits Tokyo (政府が　来年の　秋に「副首都」を　決めます)
  • Sendai University wins the Meiji Jingu Baseball Tournament, the first university champion from the Tohoku region (仙台大学が　野球の　大会で　初めて　優勝しました)
  • The US proposes new tariffs of up to 15% on Japan and 13 other economies over "excess capacity", and Japan calls it regrettable (アメリカが　日本の　物に　新しい　関税を　考えて　います)
  • Children's cafeterias reach a record 13,879 across Japan, now in over 40% of primary school districts (こども食堂が　いちばん　多く　なりました)
  • NHK's 2029 taiga drama will be "Mitsunari no Uta", with Ryunosuke Kamiki in his first lead role as Ishida Mitsunari (2029年の　大河ドラマは　神木隆之介さん)
  • Takaichi meets ICC President Akane and says Japan will pay its 2027 share to the court in full this year (日本が　ICCに　早く　お金を　払います)
  • Rakuten Securities: personal data of about 4.41 million people leaked in a cyberattack, no passwords taken (楽天証券で　お客さんの　情報が　外に　出ました)
  • Winter outlook: a warmer than usual winter is expected across Japan, but heavy snow is still possible (この　冬は　暖かく　なりそうです)
  • iDeCo private pensions: monthly limits go up from December, and people can join until age 69 (十二月から　イデコが　変わります)
  • Sumo: Onosato beats Wakamotoharu in a playoff to win the Kyushu tournament, his seventh title (大相撲　大の里が　また　優勝しました)
  • Tamagotchi turns 30, with over 100 million shipped around the world (たまごっちが　30歳に　なりました)
  • Heavy snow in Fukushima's Aizu area cuts off about 1,400 people in Kitashiobara before the roads reopen (大雪で　村の　道が　通れなく　なりました)
  • PM Takaichi and Peru's President Keiko Fujimori agree in their first talks to work together on critical minerals (高市首相と　ペルーの　大統領が　会いました)
  • Mao Shimada wins in Finland for two Grand Prix wins in her first senior season and a place in the Final (島田麻央選手が　また　優勝)
