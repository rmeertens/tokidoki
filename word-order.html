JLPT word order practice — 文の組み立て

Free, no-login practice for the JLPT grammar question where four words and particles are cut out of a sentence and you have to put them back in order (文の組み立て, the ★ question). Tap or drag the pieces into the blanks, or switch to JLPT style and answer the way the test does by picking only the piece that goes in the ★ blank. Every sentence comes with furigana, an English translation and a note on the grammar that fixes the order — 〜てから, 〜ながら, 〜ために, 〜によると, modifying clauses and more — across JLPT N5, N4 and N3. Questions come in numbered sets of five per level, with your best score kept for each set, plus a quick mixed practice where mistakes come back first.
