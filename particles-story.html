Japanese particle practice in short stories

Free, no-login particle practice where you read a short story — a day out, a part-time job, sorting the rubbish — and type the missing particle into every numbered blank yourself, instead of picking from a list. The blanks cover the particles the JLPT tests from N5 to N3: は and が, を, に and で, へ, と, も, の and や, から and まで, より, か, しか, ばかり and でも. Type in kana or in romaji (wa counts as は, e as へ and o as を). After checking, every blank comes with an explanation of why the particle fits, any other particle that would be just as right, and an English translation of the whole story, with optional furigana.
