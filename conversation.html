Japanese conversation starters: write your own answers, with hints

Free Japanese writing practice built around the questions people really ask: introduce yourself, what did you do at the weekend, what is your favourite food, why are you learning Japanese, where would you like to travel, city or countryside. Write a few sentences of your own, then open hints one step at a time — words to talk about, verbs and describing words, grammar to link your ideas (〜たり〜たり, 〜ために, 〜ようにしている, 〜ば〜ほど and more), and a way to begin. Every question has its own hints and sample answer for JLPT N5, N4 and N3, with furigana and an English translation, so you can compare and slowly learn to write longer, more interesting sentences.
