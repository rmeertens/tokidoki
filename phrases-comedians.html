Comedian catchphrases in Japanese: お笑い芸人のギャグ

One-liners from TV comedians that all of Japan copied for a year.

  • そんなの関係ねぇ！ — That's got nothing to do with it! Kojima Yoshio, 2007, stamping in his swimming trunks. Ends with オッパッピー.
  • ゲッツ！ — Get's! Dandy Sakano's finger-guns, in a yellow suit.
  • なんでだろう〜 — Why is that~ Tetsu and Tomo, 2003, singing about everyday mysteries.
  • 間違いない！ — No doubt about it! Nagai Masaru's deadpan "fun facts".
  • グ〜！ — Goo~! Edo Harumi's thumbs-up, the 2008 buzzword of the year.
  • ダメよ〜、ダメダメ。 — No~, no, no. Nihon Elekitel Rengo, the 2014 buzzword of the year.
  • 欧米か！ — What, are you Western?! Taka and Toshi: the comeback when someone acts too "foreign".
  • ルネッサ〜ンス！ — Renaissance~! Hige Danshaku, the "baron", clinking wine glasses (full of juice).
  • ちょっと何言ってるかわからない。 — I don't quite get what you're saying. Sandwichman's Tomizawa. Used when someone says something perfectly clear.
  • 35億。 — 3.5 billion. Blouson Chiemi, 2017: "how many men are on Earth? 3.5 billion" — for anyone just dumped.

Story: 文化祭の漫才 (Comedy at the school festival)

文化祭で、ケンとタロウは漫才をしました。 (At the school festival, Ken and Taro did a comedy double act.) タロウ: ぼく、朝ごはんはいつもパンとコーヒーなんです。 (I always have bread and coffee for breakfast.) ケン: 欧米か！ (What, are you Western?!) タロウ: 今日、宿題を忘れました。でも… (I forgot my homework today. But…) 二人: そんなの関係ねぇ！ (That's got nothing to do with it!) 誰も笑いません。会場はシーン… (Nobody laughs. Dead silence in the hall…) タロウ: なんでだろう〜 (Why is that~) ケン: ちょっと何言ってるかわからない。 (I don't quite get what you're saying.) 最後に、先生だけが親指を上げました。 (At the end, only the teacher gave a thumbs-up.) 先生: グ〜！ (Goo~!)
