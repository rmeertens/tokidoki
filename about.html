About me

Who makes Tokidoki, and why.

Hi, I'm Roland

I'm Roland Meertens, and Tokidoki is my side project: a free site for practising Japanese a little at a time, now and then — 時々.

It began as conjugation drills for the Genki I & II textbooks and kept growing into whatever seemed missing: grammar and particle quizzes, kana and kanji practice, graded stories, JLPT-style listening questions and three easy news stories every day, each told at N5, N4 and N3.

What I care about

  • Free, without an account. Every exercise works straight away. Logging in is optional and only syncs your progress between devices.
  • Practice, not reading about practice. Each page puts a question in front of you first, and spaced repetition brings back what you keep missing.
  • Real Japanese. Furigana on every kanji, notes on the grammar each sentence uses, and recorded voices (VOICEVOX) for listening and the news.
