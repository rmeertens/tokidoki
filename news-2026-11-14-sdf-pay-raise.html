Law raising Self-Defense Force pay is passed; new recruits from high school will get 250,300 yen a month

N5: 自衛官の　給料が　上がります

自衛官の　給料が　上がります。 (The pay of Self-Defense Force members will go up.) 十三日、国会で　新しい　法律が　できました。 (On the 13th, a new law was made in the Diet.) 高校を　出て　入った　人の　給料は、1か月　25万300円に　なります。 (For people who joined after high school, the pay will be 250,300 yen a month.) 前より　1万800円　多いです。 (That is 10,800 yen more than before.) 30歳から　40歳ぐらいの　人も、1年で　20万円ぐらい　多く　もらいます。 (People in their 30s and 40s will also get about 200,000 yen more a year.) 仕事で　引っ越す　とき、新しく　お金も　もらえます。 (When they move house for work, they can also get new money.) 小泉　防衛大臣の　話では、今までで　一番　高い　給料です。 (According to Defense Minister Koizumi, it is the highest pay ever.)

N4: 自衛官の給料を上げる法律が成立

自衛官の給料を上げる法律が、十三日の参議院の本会議で成立しました。 (A law raising the pay of Self-Defense Force members was passed at a plenary session of the House of Councillors on the 13th.) 民間の会社との給料の差を小さくするための改正で、基本給は平均で約3.4%上がります。 (The change is meant to narrow the pay gap with private companies, and basic pay will rise by about 3.4% on average.) 高校を卒業して入隊する「二士」の基本給は、1万800円増えて、月25万300円になります。 (The basic pay of a "2nd class private" who joins after finishing high school will rise by 10,800 yen to 250,300 yen a month.) 30代から40代の自衛官も、年収が20万円ぐらい増えるということです。 (Members in their 30s and 40s will also earn about 200,000 yen more a year.) 防衛省によると、高校を出て入隊した人が一生でもらう給料は、初めて3億円を超える見込みです。 (According to the Defense Ministry, the total pay over a lifetime for someone who joined after high school is expected to top 300 million yen for the first time.) 仕事で引っ越すときにもらえる「転居手当」も新しく作られました。 (A new "relocation allowance", paid when members move house for work, was also created.) 小泉防衛大臣は、すべての自衛官の給料が今までで一番高くなると説明しました。 (Defense Minister Koizumi explained that the pay of every member will be the highest ever.)

N3: 自衛官給与引き上げ法が成立　高卒二士は月25万円超に

自衛官の給与を引き上げる改正防衛省職員給与法が十三日、参院本会議で可決、成立した。 (The revised Defense Ministry Personnel Pay Act, which raises the pay of Self-Defense Force members, was approved and enacted at a plenary session of the House of Councillors on the 13th.) 人事院勧告を踏まえ、民間企業との給与差を縮めるのが狙いで、基本給は月額で平均約3.4%引き上げられる。 (Based on the National Personnel Authority's recommendation, it aims to narrow the pay gap with private companies, and monthly basic pay will be raised by about 3.4% on average.) 高卒で入隊する二士の基本給は、月額1万800円増の25万300円となる。 (The basic pay of a 2nd class private who joins after high school will rise by 10,800 yen a month to 250,300 yen.) 30代から40代の隊員も、年収が約20万円増える見通しだ。 (Members in their 30s and 40s are also expected to earn about 200,000 yen more a year.) 防衛省の試算では、高卒の二士で入隊した場合の生涯賃金が初めて3億円を超えるという。 (By the Defense Ministry's estimate, lifetime earnings for someone who joins as a 2nd class private after high school will top 300 million yen for the first time.) 転勤に伴う引っ越しを対象とした「転居手当」も新設された。 (A "relocation allowance" for moves that come with a transfer was also created.) 小泉防衛相は、全自衛官の給与が過去最高の水準になると説明した。 (Defense Minister Koizumi explained that the pay of all Self-Defense Force members will reach its highest level ever.)
