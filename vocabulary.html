Vocabulary Hover Reference

A browsable JLPT vocabulary list, covering N5 through N3. Each card shows a Japanese word and its English meaning stacked, one on top of the other, but only one side is visible at first — hover over a card to peek at the hidden side, or click it to reveal that side permanently. Switch the direction to show the word first and hide the meaning, or show the meaning first and hide the word, and choose how the Japanese word is written: with kanji, with furigana readings above the kanji, or spelled out entirely in hiragana/katakana. Filter which JLPT levels appear. Pairs well with the Kanji Hover reference and the printable practice sheets.
