Vocabulary Hover Reference

A browsable JLPT vocabulary list, covering N5 through N3. Each card shows a Japanese word and its English meaning stacked, one on top of the other, but only one side is visible at first — hover over a card to peek at the hidden side, or click it to reveal that side permanently. Switch the direction to show the word first and hide the meaning, or show the meaning first and hide the word, and choose how the Japanese word is written: with kanji, with furigana readings above the kanji, or spelled out entirely in hiragana/katakana. Filter which JLPT levels appear. The Picture → Word direction shows a drawing for each word that can be pictured — foods, animals, clothes, places, the body, colours — and hides the Japanese word until you hover or click. Typing practice goes through the same words one at a time: type the English meaning, or type the Japanese word in hiragana (romaji turns into kana as you type, and Space turns the kana into kanji), and see straight away whether it was right. Any word can be added to the same personal flashcard deck as the Stories page, where it is reviewed with spaced repetition, and words already in the deck get an orange outline. Pairs well with the Kanji Hover reference and the printable practice sheets.
