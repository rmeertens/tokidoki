Confusable Kanji Groups

A reference list of JLPT kanji (N5 through N1) that look alike enough to mix up at a glance — grouped by comparing rendered glyph shapes for visual overlap, plus a small set of hand-checked pairs the automatic comparison under-scored. 222 groups covering 601 of the 2,213 kanji in the practice pool, sorted by hardest JLPT level then group size. Used as the distractor source for the Confusing Kanji multiple-choice quiz, where you're shown one kanji from a group and asked to pick its correct meaning out of four choices drawn from that group.
