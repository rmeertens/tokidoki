Everyday set phrases in Japanese: 毎日の決まり文句

Little phrases with no real English translation — Japan runs on them.

  • いただきます！ — (I humbly receive.) Before eating, hands together. Thanks to everyone — and everything — that made the meal.
  • ごちそうさまでした。 — That was a feast. After eating. Say it to the cook, or to the staff as you leave a small restaurant.
  • いってきます！ — I'm off (and I'll be back)! Leaving home. The family answers いってらっしゃい！
  • ただいま！ — I'm home! Coming home. The answer: おかえり（なさい）！
  • お疲れさまです。 — You must be tired (thanks for your work). The all-purpose work greeting: hello, goodbye and thank you in one.
  • よろしくお願いします。 — Please treat me well. After introducing yourself, asking a favour, starting anything together.
  • お先に失礼します。 — Excuse me for leaving before you. Leaving the office while others are still working.
  • お邪魔します。 — I'm going to disturb you. Stepping into someone's home. When you leave: お邪魔しました。
  • いえいえ、とんでもないです。 — No, no, not at all. The polite way to brush off a compliment. Accepting it straight sounds boastful.
  • つまらないものですが… — It's just a boring little thing, but… Said while handing over a gift. Nowadays many people say ほんの気持ちですが "just a token" instead.
  • しょうがない。 — It can't be helped. Rain on the picnic, a missed train, life. Also しかたない.
  • 気をつけて！ — Take care! / Watch out! When someone leaves, or is about to trip over something.

Story: ケンの一日 (Ken's day in set phrases)

朝、ケンは家を出ます。 (In the morning, Ken leaves home.) ケン: いってきます！ (I'm off!) お母さん: いってらっしゃい！気をつけて！ (See you later! Take care!) 会社では、みんなが言います。 (At the office, everyone says:) みんな: お疲れさまです。 (Thanks for your hard work.) 夜、ケンは友達の家に行きました。 (In the evening, Ken went to a friend's home.) ケン: お邪魔します。これ、つまらないものですが… (Sorry to disturb you. This is just a boring little thing, but…) 二人は手を合わせました。 (The two of them put their hands together.) 二人: いただきます！ (Let's eat!) 家に帰ると、お母さんが待っていました。 (When he got home, his mother was waiting.) ケン: ただいま！ (I'm home!)
