JLPT Kanji Quiz & Flashcards

Free, no-login kanji flashcard quiz covering JLPT N5 through N1. Test yourself in either direction — see the meaning and recall the kanji, or see the kanji and recall its meaning — then self-grade against the revealed answer. Progress is scheduled with spaced repetition (SRS) per direction, so you review what you find hardest instead of what you already know. Uses the same kanji lists and order as the printable practice sheets.
