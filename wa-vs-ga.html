は vs が: which particle?

Free, no login. Warm up with 6 quick questions, or open the full particle quiz →

The rule of thumb

は

The topic: what the sentence is about, something you both already know.

  • 私(わたし)は学生(がくせい)です。I am a student.
  • これは私(わたし)の傘(かさ)です。This is my umbrella.

が

The new information: existence, ability, likes, and the answer to a question word like 誰 or 何.

  • 猫(ねこ)がいます。There is a cat.
  • 誰(だれ)が来(き)ましたか。Who came?
  • 日本語(にほんご)がわかります。I understand Japanese.

Both often share one sentence: in 私(わたし)は寿司(すし)が好(す)きです。 ("I like sushi") 私 is the topic and 寿司 is the thing that is liked.
