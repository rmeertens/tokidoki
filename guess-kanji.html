    GTK - Easy Word Order — JLPT words in an order you can guess

    Most Japanese words are built from kanji you will meet again and again, read the same way: 電 is でん in 電車 (train) and in 電気 (electricity), 気 is き in 天気 (weather) and in 電気. GTK - Easy Word Order puts the JLPT N5, N4 and N3 vocabulary in an order where each kanji arrives in a plain word first, so the words built from it can be guessed — reading and meaning — on first sight instead of memorised. The order is a greedy path, after GuessWithAnki, planned again in your browser from the words you have already studied, and every word is reviewed with spaced repetition.
