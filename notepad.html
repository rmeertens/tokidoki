Japanese Notepad: type romaji, get kanji with furigana

A free online Japanese input method for learners. Type in romaji and pick the kanji, hiragana or katakana spelling from a list of suggestions, or type an English word to find its Japanese equivalent. Full sentences like “kyou wa ame ga futte imasu” are split into words automatically, conjugated verbs and adjectives are recognised, and the result is displayed with furigana (reading aids) above every kanji. Suggestions come from the JLPT N5–N3 vocabulary list and the words in the graded stories. Put English words or names between double quotes to mix them into the Japanese text unchanged. Click a sentence to add its words to your flashcards (saved in their dictionary form, with the sentence they came from). Copy the text as plain Japanese or with readings.
