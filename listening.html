    JLPT listening practice (聴解) with picture choices

    Free, no-login listening practice in the format of the JLPT N5 and N4 listening section (聴解). Each question is played with Japanese text-to-speech the way the test plays it: the question number, the situation and question, a short conversation between a man and a woman, and the question again. 問題1 課題理解 (task comprehension) and 問題2 ポイント理解 (point comprehension) are answered by picking one of four pictures — clocks, calendars, maps, people, weather and shopping. 問題3 発話表現 (utterance expressions) shows a picture with an arrow and three spoken replies, and 問題4 即時応答 (quick response) has no picture at all.

    After answering, read the script with furigana and English translations and tap any line to hear it again. Options: slower speech, exam mode where the audio plays only once, and showing the script while listening.
