    JLPT listening practice (聴解) with picture choices

    Free, no-login listening practice in the format of the JLPT N5, N4, N3 and N2 listening sections (聴解): 438 questions with natural recorded voices — fifteen different speakers, young and old, at different speeds — and fifteen full mock tests (five N5, four N4, three N3, three N2) in the layout of the real test. Each question plays the way the test does — the question number, the situation and question, a short conversation between a man and a woman, and the question again. 問題1 課題理解 (task comprehension) and 問題2 ポイント理解 (point comprehension) are answered by picking one of four pictures: clocks, calendars, maps and routes, cinema seats, floors of a department store, a room layout, people's clothes, buses and platforms, food, shopping and weather. 問題3 発話表現 (utterance expressions) shows a scene — a shop, a classroom, a train, a home entrance — with an arrow over the speaker and three spoken replies, and 問題4 即時応答 (quick response) has no picture at all. At N3 and N2 the choices become printed phrases, and 概要理解 (summary comprehension) asks what a whole talk was about, with spoken choices only.

    After answering, read the script with furigana and English translations and tap any line to hear it again. Practise each part in fixed sets of five questions, filter by level (N5 to N2), review the questions you missed, change the speed, or turn on exam mode, where the audio plays once and you have 12 seconds to answer.
