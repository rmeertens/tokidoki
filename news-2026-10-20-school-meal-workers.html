Government plans to resume accepting foreign workers for hospital and care-facility meal services under a different category

N5: 病院のごはんを作る外国人を、また受け入れます

日本の国は、外国から来て働く人について、新しい案を出しました。 (Japan's government has put out a new plan about people who come from abroad to work.) 今、レストランなどの仕事では、新しい外国人を受け入れていません。 (At the moment, new foreign workers are not being accepted for jobs such as restaurants.) でも、病院や、お年寄りの施設で、ごはんを作る仕事は、人が足りません。 (But hospitals and facilities for older people do not have enough people to cook meals.) それで、この仕事は別の分野にして、また外国人を受け入れる案です。 (So the plan is to put this work in a different field and accept foreign workers again.) 来年の4月から始める予定だと言われています。 (It is reported that it is planned to start in April next year.) まだ、決まっていません。 (It has not been decided yet.)

N4: 病院や施設の給食、外国人の受け入れを再開する案

政府は十九日、外国人が働ける「特定技能」という制度について、新しい案を専門家の会議に示しました。 (On the 19th, the government showed a panel of experts a new plan about the "Specified Skilled Worker" system, which lets foreigners work in Japan.) 今年の4月から、「外食業」では新しい人の受け入れが止まっています。 (Since April this year, accepting new people has been stopped in the "restaurant industry" field.) 2029年3月までの上限の5万人を超えそうだからです。 (This is because the limit of 50,000 people through March 2029 is likely to be exceeded.) 案は、病院や介護施設の給食の仕事を、食品工場などの「飲食料品製造業」の分野に移すものです。 (The plan would move meal-service work at hospitals and care facilities to the "food and beverage manufacturing" field, which includes food factories.) 入院している人などの食事を出せなくなるおそれがあるからです。 (This is because there is a risk that meals could no longer be served to people in hospital and others.) 来年の4月から受け入れを再開する方向で、政府は年内に決めたいと考えています。 (The aim is to resume accepting workers from April next year, and the government wants to decide within the year.)

N3: 医療・福祉施設の給食業、外国人の受け入れ再開へ　特定技能の分野を変更案

政府は十九日、有識者会議で、在留資格「特定技能」の「外食業」分野のうち、病院や介護施設などの給食業務を別の分野で受け入れ直す案を示した。 (At a panel of experts on the 19th, the government presented a plan to accept meal-service work at hospitals, care facilities and the like, which falls under the "restaurant industry" field of the "Specified Skilled Worker" residence status, under a different field.) 外食業分野では、2029年3月末までの上限5万人を超えると見込まれるため、今年4月から新規の受け入れが止まっている。 (In the restaurant industry field, accepting new workers has been stopped since April this year because the cap of 50,000 people through the end of March 2029 is expected to be exceeded.) このままでは入院患者らへの食事の提供が難しくなるおそれがあるとして、政府は対応を検討してきた。 (Seeing a risk that, as things stand, it could become difficult to provide meals to hospital patients and others, the government has been considering a response.) 案では、給食業務を食品工場などが対象の「飲食料品製造業」分野に移す。 (Under the plan, meal-service work would be moved to the "food and beverage manufacturing" field, which covers food factories and the like.) こうした施設では、食材の下ごしらえや調理が中心で、接客の割合が低い職場が多いという。 (At these facilities, the work is mainly preparing and cooking ingredients, and many workplaces are said to involve little dealing with customers.) 来年4月ごろからの再開を目指し、政府は年内の閣議決定を目指す方針だ。 (Aiming to resume around April next year, the government intends to seek a Cabinet decision within the year.) 外食業そのものの受け入れ停止は、当面続くとみられる。 (The halt on accepting workers in the restaurant industry itself is expected to continue for the time being.)
