Food & drinking in Japanese: 食べ物と飲み会

For the izakaya, the ramen shop and the dessert counter.

  • とりあえずビール！ — Beer, for starters! The first order at every izakaya, before anyone has read the menu.
  • デザートは別腹。 — Dessert goes in a separate stomach. Full of dinner, but there's always room for cake.
  • シメはラーメンで。 — Let's finish with ramen. シメ (〆) = the closing dish of the night: ramen after drinks, rice in the hotpot.
  • おかわり！ — Seconds, please! Some ramen shops let you order a 替え玉 — an extra ball of noodles for your soup.
  • 私、猫舌なんです。 — I have a cat's tongue. You can't handle hot food or drinks, like a cat.
  • 食べ放題、飲み放題！ — All you can eat, all you can drink! 〜放題 = as much as you like, usually for a set time.
  • 一口ちょうだい！ — Give me a bite! Sharing food is common among friends.
  • いい食べっぷりだね！ — You really eat with gusto! A compliment — Japanese hosts love a big eater.
  • 花より団子 — Dumplings over flowers. At cherry-blossom parties, the food is the real star. A proverb for practical people.

Story: 居酒屋で (At the izakaya)

仕事のあと、四人で居酒屋に行きました。 (After work, four of them went to an izakaya.) 店員: ご注文は？ (What would you like?) みんな: とりあえずビール！ (Beer, for starters!) メアリーは熱いたこ焼きを食べました。 (Mary ate a hot takoyaki.) メアリー: あつっ！私、猫舌なんです。 (Hot! I have a cat's tongue.) 先輩: ケンくん、いい食べっぷりだね！ (Ken, you really eat with gusto!) 十時、みんなおなかがいっぱいです。でも、メアリーはアイスを頼みました。 (At ten, everyone is full. But Mary orders ice cream.) メアリー: デザートは別腹です。 (Dessert goes in a separate stomach.) ケン: じゃあ、シメはラーメンで！ (Then let's finish with ramen!) みんな: まだ食べるの？ (You're still eating?!)
