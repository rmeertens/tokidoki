Making friends in Japanese: 友達を作ろう

Casual Japanese for hanging out.

  • タメ口でいいよ。 — You can talk casually with me. An invitation to drop です/ます — a real step towards friendship.
  • なんて呼べばいい？ — What should I call you? First name, nickname, or name + ちゃん / くん?
  • 週末、ひま？ — Free this weekend? ひま = free time. Very casual.
  • 今度、遊ぼうよ！ — Let's hang out sometime! 遊ぶ isn't only for kids — adults "play" together too.
  • カラオケ行かない？ — Want to go to karaoke? A negative question is the friendly way to invite.
  • いいね！行こう行こう！ — Nice! Let's go, let's go! Repeating it sounds keen.
  • それな！ — Right?! / Exactly! Young people's "I totally agree".
  • マジで？ — Seriously? マジ = serious. マジでうまい = "seriously tasty".
  • 一緒に写真撮ろう！ — Let's take a photo together! Peace sign optional — but expected.
  • 私たち、気が合うね！ — We really get along! 気が合う = your spirits match.

Story: カラオケに行こう (Let's go to karaoke)

ユキとメアリーは、同じクラスです。 (Yuki and Mary are in the same class.) メアリー: ユキさん、週末、ひまですか？ (Yuki, are you free this weekend?) ユキ: ひまだよ！あ、タメ口でいいよ。 (I'm free! Oh — you can talk casually with me.) メアリー: じゃあ…カラオケ行かない？ (Then… want to go to karaoke?) ユキ: いいね！行こう行こう！ (Nice! Let's go, let's go!) カラオケで、二人は三時間も歌いました。 (At karaoke the two of them sang for three whole hours.) メアリー: 一緒に写真撮ろう！ (Let's take a photo together!) ユキ: 私たち、気が合うね！ (We really get along!) メアリー: それな！ (Right?!)
