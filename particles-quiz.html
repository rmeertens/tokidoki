Japanese Particle Quiz

Free, no-login multiple-choice quiz for Japanese particles — は, が, を, に, で, へ, と, も, の, and や — with 200 example sentences across the ten particles. Each question shows a sentence with one particle missing and its English translation; pick the particle that fits out of four choices and get graded instantly, with an explanation of the grammar rule whenever you get it wrong. Choose which particles to include so you can drill the ones you keep mixing up, like に vs で vs へ, or は vs が. Progress is scheduled with spaced repetition (SRS) per sentence, so you review what you find hardest instead of what you already know.
