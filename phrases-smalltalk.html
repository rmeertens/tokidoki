Seasonal small talk in Japanese: 季節のあいさつ

Japan talks about the weather — and the seasons — a lot.

  • 暑いですねえ。 — Hot, isn't it? Summer's number-one conversation starter. Winter: 寒いですねえ。
  • お花見しよう！ — Let's go cherry-blossom viewing! Late March to early April: picnics under the trees, often all day.
  • 花粉症がつらい。 — My hay fever is awful. Cedar pollen hits roughly a third of Japan every spring. Masks everywhere.
  • 梅雨入りしましたね。 — The rainy season has started. June to mid-July. 梅雨 = "plum rain", the season the plums ripen.
  • 夏バテしちゃった。 — The summer heat has worn me out. The cure: eel (うなぎ) on the Day of the Ox in late July.
  • 紅葉がきれいですね。 — The autumn leaves are beautiful. Going out to see them is 紅葉狩り — "autumn-leaf hunting".
  • こたつから出られない… — I can't get out of the kotatsu… The heated table with a blanket — the most dangerous furniture in winter.
  • 年越しそば、食べた？ — Did you have your year-crossing soba? Long noodles on New Year's Eve for a long life.

Story: となりの田中さん (Mrs Tanaka next door)

となりの田中さんは、会うといつも季節の話をします。 (Mrs Tanaka next door always talks about the season when we meet.) 田中さん: 花粉症がつらいですねえ。 (My hay fever is awful.) それは三月でした。六月には… (That was in March. In June…) 田中さん: 梅雨入りしましたね。 (The rainy season has started.) 八月には… (In August…) 田中さん: 暑いですねえ。夏バテしちゃった。 (Hot, isn't it? The heat has worn me out.) 十一月には… (In November…) 田中さん: 紅葉がきれいですね。 (The autumn leaves are beautiful.) 一月、田中さんは外に出てきませんでした。こたつから出られないそうです。 (In January, Mrs Tanaka didn't come out. Apparently she can't get out of the kotatsu.) 一年後、気がつくと私も同じ話をしています。 (A year later, I realise I talk exactly the same way.)
