A first date in Japanese: 初デート

From asking someone out to "text me when you get home".

  • 今度、ご飯でも行きませんか？ — Want to get dinner sometime? でも softens it: "dinner or something". The classic low-pressure invite.
  • どこで待ち合わせする？ — Where shall we meet up? Popular Tokyo spot: the Hachiko statue at Shibuya — so popular you can't find anyone there.
  • 改札の前で待ってるね。 — I'll wait in front of the ticket gates. Make sure it's the right exit — big stations have dozens.
  • ごめん、ちょっと遅れる！ — Sorry, I'll be a bit late! Send it before the meeting time, not after. Punctuality matters.
  • 緊張してる。 — I'm nervous. Admitting it is cute, not weak.
  • その服、似合ってるね。 — That outfit suits you. 似合う = to suit. A safe compliment.
  • 何か苦手な食べ物ある？ — Any food you don't like? 苦手 = not good with — a softer word than 嫌い (hate).
  • 割り勘にしようか。 — Shall we split the bill? Common among young couples. The other option…
  • ここは私が払うよ。 — Let me get this. …and the polite fight over the bill that follows.
  • 今日はすごく楽しかった！ — I had a great time today! Past tense of 楽しい — say it at the station when you part.
  • また会えるかな？ — Can we meet again? かな makes it a soft, hopeful wonder.
  • 家に着いたら連絡してね。 — Message me when you get home. The caring goodbye. Then wait for the LINE.

Story: 渋谷で初デート (A first date in Shibuya)

土曜日、ケンはユキと初デートです。 (On Saturday, Ken has his first date with Yuki.) ケンは朝からずっと緊張しています。 (Ken has been nervous since the morning.) ユキからLINEが来ました。 (A LINE message came from Yuki.) ユキ: ごめん、ちょっと遅れる！ (Sorry, I'll be a bit late!) 十分後、ユキが改札から出てきました。 (Ten minutes later, Yuki came out of the ticket gates.) ケン: その服、似合ってるね。 (That outfit suits you.) ケン: 何か苦手な食べ物ある？ (Any food you don't like?) ユキ: ピーマン以外なら、大丈夫！ (Anything but green peppers!) お会計のとき、ケンが財布を出しました。 (When the bill came, Ken took out his wallet.) ケン: ここはぼくが払うよ。 (Let me get this.) ユキ: じゃあ、次は私ね。今日はすごく楽しかった！ (Then next time it's me. I had a great time today!) ケン: ぼくも。家に着いたら連絡してね。 (Me too. Message me when you get home.)
