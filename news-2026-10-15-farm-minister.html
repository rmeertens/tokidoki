Agriculture Minister Yana resigns — the first scandal-related resignation in the Takaichi cabinet

N5: 農林水産大臣が、やめました

十四日、ヤナ・カズオ農林水産大臣が、大臣をやめました。 (On the 14th, Agriculture Minister Kazuo Yana resigned as minister.) 高市総理大臣は、やめることを認めました。 (Prime Minister Takaichi accepted his resignation.) ヤナさんは、道を作るお金について、よくないことを言いました。 (Mr. Yana said something inappropriate about money for building roads.) 野党は、ヤナさんにやめてほしいと言っていました。 (The opposition parties had been saying they wanted Mr. Yana to resign.) 高市内閣で、問題のためにやめた大臣は、初めてです。 (He is the first minister in the Takaichi cabinet to resign over a problem.) 次の大臣は、武部新さんになるようです。 (It seems the next minister will be Arata Takebe.)

N4: ヤナ農林水産大臣が辞任、高市内閣で初めて

十四日、ヤナ・カズオ農林水産大臣が、高市総理大臣に辞表を出しました。 (On the 14th, Agriculture Minister Kazuo Yana submitted his letter of resignation to Prime Minister Takaichi.) 高市総理大臣は辞表を受け取り、ヤナさんを大臣に選んだ責任は重いと話しました。 (Takaichi accepted the letter and said she takes seriously her responsibility for choosing Mr. Yana as minister.) ヤナさんは、自分を応援しなかった市長の町の道路予算が減らされた、という話をしたと報じられました。 (It was reported that Mr. Yana had said that road budgets were cut for towns whose mayors did not support him.) ヤナさんは十月五日に、この話を取り消してあやまっていました。 (On October 5, Mr. Yana had taken back the remarks and apologized.) 野党は、大臣の責任を問う問責決議案を出すことを考えていました。 (The opposition parties had been considering submitting a censure motion over the minister's responsibility.) 食料品の消費税を下げる法案について、ヤナさんがやめるまで国会で話し合わないという野党もありました。 (Some opposition parties said they would not take part in talks on the bill to cut the consumption tax on food until Mr. Yana resigned.) ヤナさんは九月十七日に大臣になったばかりで、次の大臣は武部新さんになる見通しです。 (Mr. Yana had become minister on September 17, and Arata Takebe is expected to be the next minister.)

N3: ヤナ農林水産相が辞任、高市内閣で不祥事による閣僚辞任は初めて

ヤナ・カズオ農林水産相は十四日、高市早苗首相に辞表を提出し、受理された。 (Agriculture Minister Kazuo Yana submitted his resignation to Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi on the 14th, and it was accepted.) 高市首相は記者団に対し、任命した責任を重く受け止めていると述べた。 (Takaichi told reporters that she takes seriously her responsibility for having appointed him.) ヤナ氏は、自身を支持しなかった市長の自治体への道路予算を減らしたと受け取れる発言をしたと報じられ、十月五日に撤回して謝罪していた。 (Mr. Yana had been reported to have made remarks that could be taken to mean road budgets were cut for municipalities whose mayors did not support him, and on October 5 he retracted them and apologized.) このほか、選挙運動で支持者に無料のバスを提供した疑いも指摘されていた。 (In addition, it had been pointed out that he was suspected of providing free buses to supporters during his election campaign.) 野党は辞任を求め、参院では問責決議案の提出を検討していたほか、食料品の消費税を下げる法案の審議に応じない構えを見せる党もあった。 (The opposition demanded his resignation and was considering a censure motion in the upper house, and some parties also signaled they would not take part in deliberations on the bill to cut the consumption tax on food.) 高市内閣で閣僚が不祥事で辞任するのは初めてで、ヤナ氏は九月十七日の内閣改造で入閣したばかりだった。 (This is the first time a cabinet minister has resigned over a scandal in the Takaichi cabinet; Mr. Yana had joined the cabinet only in the reshuffle on September 17.) 後任には、元農林水産副大臣の武部新氏が起用される見通しだ。 (Arata Takebe, a former vice minister of agriculture, forestry and fisheries, is expected to be appointed as his successor.)
