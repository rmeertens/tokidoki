Graded Japanese Stories for JLPT N5, N4 and N3

Free, no-login graded readers for learners of Japanese, sorted by JLPT level. N5 stories use short です / ます sentences about everyday life; N4 stories link clauses with 〜たら, 〜ので, 〜てしまう and giving-and-receiving verbs; N3 stories are narrated in plain form with the passive, causative, hearsay 〜そうだ and more.
