Anime & manga lines in Japanese: アニメ・漫画の名セリフ

Catchphrases fans shout at each other.

  • 海賊王に、おれはなる！ — I'm gonna be King of the Pirates! Luffy, One Piece — the series' first big promise.
  • 真実はいつもひとつ！ — There is only ever one truth! Detective Conan, pointing at the culprit.
  • あれれ〜？おかしいぞ〜？ — Huh~? That's weird~? Conan playing the innocent kid while dropping a clue for the adults.
  • じっちゃんの名にかけて！ — On my grandpa's name! Kindaichi Case Files: his grandfather was the famous detective Kosuke Kindaichi.
  • 月に代わって、おしおきよ！ — In the name of the moon, I'll punish you! Sailor Moon. Literally "standing in for the moon".
  • かめはめ波ー！ — Kamehameha wave! Goku's signature attack, Dragon Ball — named after the Hawaiian king.
  • クリリンのことかーっ！ — You mean Krillin?! Goku turning Super Saiyan for the first time. Used when something makes you snap.
  • お前のものはおれのもの、おれのものもおれのもの。 — What's yours is mine, and what's mine is mine too. Gian, the bully in Doraemon. Japan's name for this attitude: ジャイアニズム.
  • のび太のくせに、生意気だぞ！ — You're just Nobita — don't get cocky! Gian again. 〜のくせに = "for someone who's only…".
  • 親父にもぶたれたことないのに！ — Not even my father ever hit me! Amuro, Mobile Suit Gundam (1979), after being slapped.
  • 立て、立つんだジョー！ — Get up — get up, Joe! The coach in the boxing manga Ashita no Joe. Quoted to anyone on the floor.
  • 心を燃やせ。 — Set your heart ablaze. Rengoku, Demon Slayer (鬼滅の刃).
  • 全集中！ — Total concentration! The breathing technique in Demon Slayer. Kids say it before a test.
  • 領域展開。 — Domain expansion. Jujutsu Kaisen's ultimate technique — and a meme for taking over a room.
  • 私が来た！ — I am here! All Might, My Hero Academia, arriving to save the day.
  • わくわく！ — So exciting! Anya in Spy×Family, eyes sparkling.
  • 心臓を捧げよ！ — Dedicate your hearts! The Survey Corps salute in Attack on Titan, fist on chest.
  • ピカチュウ、きみにきめた！ — Pikachu, I choose you! Satoshi (Ash), Pokémon. In Japanese, literally "I've decided on you".

Story: アニメ部の一日 (A day at the anime club)

アニメ部の三人は、毎日セリフで話します。 (The three anime club members talk in anime lines every day.) ケン: 海賊王に、おれはなる！ (I'm gonna be King of the Pirates!) ユキ: まず、宿題をしてね。 (Do your homework first.) テストの前、ケンは目を閉じました。 (Before the test, Ken closed his eyes.) ケン: 全集中… (Total concentration…) でも、答えは一つもわかりませんでした。 (But he didn't know a single answer.) 昼ごはんのとき、ケンのパンがなくなりました。 (At lunch, Ken's bread disappeared.) ケン: あれれ〜？おかしいぞ〜？ (Huh~? That's weird~?) ユキ: 犯人は…タロウくん！真実はいつもひとつ！ (The culprit is… Taro! There is only ever one truth!) タロウ: お前のものはおれのもの、おれのものもおれのもの。 (What's yours is mine, and what's mine is mine too.) ケン: ジャイアンか！ (Who are you, Gian?!)
