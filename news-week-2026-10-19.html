Easy Japanese news: 19 October – 25 October 2026

15 stories from Japan, each told in simple Japanese at JLPT N5, N4 and N3.

  • Revised Imperial House Law takes effect: women of the imperial family can keep their status after marriage (皇室の新しい法律が始まりました)
  • Hideki Matsui named new manager of the Yomiuri Giants (松井秀喜さんが巨人の新しい監督になります)
  • Large Typhoon Champi passes Okinawa and may approach Kyushu on Monday (台風30号が沖縄の近くを通りました)
  • Japan wins men's team gold and women's team bronze at the gymnastics world championships (体操の世界大会　日本が金メダルと銅メダル)
  • Japan's exports rise 16.7% in September, driven by AI-related chips (9月の輸出　去年より16.7%ふえました)
  • Family of driver who died in Saitama road collapse sues the prefecture for about ¥125 million (八潮の道の事故　家族が県をうったえました)
  • Obstetricians' society to stop using the term "abnormal delivery" when explaining births to mothers (「異常分娩」という言葉を、使わないことにしました)
  • Data on about 125,000 police officers and staff may have leaked after unauthorized access to a cloud service (警察の人の情報が、外に出たかもしれません)
  • Japan to provide Malaysia's navy with a ¥1.65 billion counter-drone system under its security aid program (日本がマレーシアに、ドローンをふせぐ機械をあげます)
  • H3 rocket launches Japan's MMX probe toward Mars's moon Phobos (火星の月へ行くロケットが、飛びました)
  • Lawyer Atsumi Harasawa wins the Yokohama mayoral election after the previous mayor resigned over power harassment (横浜に新しい市長が決まりました)
  • Government plans to resume accepting foreign workers for hospital and care-facility meal services under a different category (病院のごはんを作る外国人を、また受け入れます)
  • Flu spreads early: 12.81 cases per clinic nationwide, about five times last year (インフルエンザが早くふえています)
  • Consumption tax cut bill to enter Diet debate on October 20, with Takaichi attending (税金を安くする法律の話が始まります)
  • "My Number Card on Android" to launch on October 20 (スマホでマイナンバーカードが使えます)
