Government shows a rough figure of about 5,000 yen a year for an income-linked benefit for low earners

N5: おかねが　すくない　人に　5000円ぐらい

国は、おかねが　すくない　人に、おかねを　あげる　ことを　考えています。 (The government is thinking of giving money to people who have little money.) 三十日に、国会で　その　かんがえを　見せました。 (On the 30th, it showed this idea in the Diet.) 一年に　一人　5000円ぐらいです。 (It is about 5,000 yen per person per year.) 一年の　おかねが　119万円までの　人が　もらえます。 (People whose yearly income is up to 1.19 million yen can get it.) 来年の　秋ごろに　はじまります。 (It will start around autumn next year.) でも、まだ　きまっていません。 (But it has not been decided yet.)

N4: 低所得の人に年5000円ほどの給付を検討

政府は三十日、国会の委員会に、新しい給付の資料を出しました。 (On the 30th the government gave a Diet committee material on a new benefit.) 収入が少ない人に、お金を配る仕組みです。 (It is a system to hand out money to people with low incomes.) 年収が119万円までの人に、同じ金額を出します。 (The same amount is paid to people whose annual income is up to 1.19 million yen.) 金額は、一人一年で5000円ほどが目安です。 (The guide for the amount is about 5,000 yen per person per year.) 食べ物の消費税を下げる法案も、国会に出されています。 (A bill to lower the consumption tax on food has also been submitted to the Diet.) 給付は来年の秋ごろに始まる予定ですが、収入の条件はまだ決まっていません。 (The benefit is planned to start around autumn next year, but the income conditions are not yet decided.)

N3: 低所得者への所得連動給付　政府が年5000円程度を参考値に

政府は三十日、衆議院の財務金融委員会に、所得に応じた給付の資料を示した。 (On the 30th the government presented to the House of Representatives Committee on Finance material on a benefit based on income.) 来年秋ごろの開始を目指し、年収119万円までの低所得者に定額を給付する。 (Aiming to start around autumn next year, it would pay a fixed amount to low earners with annual income of up to 1.19 million yen.) 金額は、一人に年5000円程度が参考になるという。 (It is said that about 5,000 yen per person per year is the reference figure.) 食料品の消費税1%分にあたる約6000億円を、人口で割った額が根拠とされる。 (The basis is said to be the amount obtained by dividing about 600 billion yen, equal to 1% of the consumption tax on food, by the population.) 政府は、食料品の消費税を2027年4月から二年間、1%下げる法案を国会に提出している。 (The government has submitted to the Diet a bill to cut the consumption tax on food by 1% for two years from April 2027.) 所得の基準は政令で定める予定で、内容は今後変わる可能性がある。 (The income standard is to be set by a government ordinance, and the details may change in the future.)
