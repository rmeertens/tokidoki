JLPT passage grammar practice — 文章の文法

Free, no-login practice for the JLPT grammar question where you read a longer passage — an essay, a letter, a short story — and choose the best word for each numbered blank from four choices. The blanks test what the JLPT tests: particles, conjunctions like でも, だから, それに and その結果, giving and receiving verbs, verb forms, and grammar such as 〜そうです, 〜によると, 〜だけでなく and 〜をきっかけに. After checking, every blank comes with an explanation of why the answer fits and the other choices don't, plus an English translation of the whole passage. Passages for JLPT N5, N4 and N3, with optional furigana.
