異議あり！

Kuro the Word Detective — a Japanese mystery game

ことば探偵クロ (Kotoba Tantei Kuro) is a free, no-login detective story for Japanese learners, inspired by puzzle-adventure and courtroom games. Kuro, a black cat detective, speaks only Japanese; you play his assistant and move the story on by answering in Japanese. Challenges include fill-in-the-blank lines (particles, conjugations, counters, time), whole sentences to type (questions for suspects, polite thanks, humble apologies), なぞなぞ riddles and kanji-building puzzles like 木 + 木 = 林, and cross-examinations where you find the statement that contradicts the evidence and shout 異議あり.

Ten cases grow harder from JLPT N5 to N3. Part one: The Vanishing Melon Pan (particles, days of the week, あります and います), The Stopped Clock Tower (telling time, て-form, い-adjective past), The Moon Tea Bowl (keigo, 〜ている vs 〜てある, 〜てしまう) and Kuro Arrested?! (hearsay そうだ, passive vs causative, 〜ておく, 申し訳ありません). Part two follows the phantom thief Mike through the festival weekend: homophones and 〜べからず, keigo mistakes in a forged letter, alibis decided by 〜ところ, Kansai dialect, the causative-passive, 〜わけだ and 〜ものの. Type in romaji and it is converted to hiragana; kanji and kana spellings are both accepted. As on the Stories page, tap any word for its reading and meaning and save it to your flashcard deck, or tap a line to see the grammar it uses.
