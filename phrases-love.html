Love & compliments in Japanese: 恋とほめことば

Confessions, compliments and the trope-words of romance manga.

  • 月が綺麗ですね。 — The moon is beautiful, isn't it? Legend says Natsume Soseki taught that a Japanese person would say "I love you" this way.
  • 好きです。付き合ってください！ — I like you. Please go out with me! The 告白 (confession): couples officially start with this.
  • 一目惚れしました。 — It was love at first sight. Literally "fell for you in one look".
  • 胸キュン！ — Heart-squeeze! That tight little feeling in your chest in a romantic scene.
  • 壁ドン — Wall-slam The manga move: hand on the wall, trapping your crush. ドン is the slam.
  • 結婚してください。 — Please marry me. The proposal line — simple and direct.
  • センスいいね！ — You've got good taste! センス = a sense of style.
  • かっこいい！ — So cool! For a stylish person, a nice car, a great move.
  • 日本語、上手ですね！ — Your Japanese is so good! You'll hear it after saying ありがとう. Reply いえいえ、まだまだです (no, I've got a long way to go).

Story: 月が綺麗ですね (The moon is beautiful)

ケンとユキは、夜の公園を歩いています。 (Ken and Yuki are walking through the park at night.) ケンはとても緊張しています。 (Ken is very nervous.) ケン: ユキさん…つ、月が綺麗ですね。 (Yuki… th-the moon is beautiful, isn't it?) ユキ: え？今日、くもりだよ。 (Huh? It's cloudy tonight.) 空を見ると、月はどこにもありません。 (They look up. There's no moon anywhere.) ケン: …あの、好きです。付き合ってください！ (…Um, I like you. Please go out with me!) ユキ: 最初からそう言ってよ。…うん、いいよ。 (Should have said that in the first place. …Yes, OK.) ケンの顔は真っ赤になりました。 (Ken's face went bright red.) ユキ: 胸キュン！ (Heart-squeeze!)
