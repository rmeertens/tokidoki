Japanese vocabulary quiz by topic (JLPT N5–N3)

Test yourself on the JLPT N5, N4 and N3 vocabulary lists, sorted into topic sets of 20 to 50 words: numbers and time, food and drink, the body and health, people and family, school, places and transport, nature and weather, verbs of moving, daily life, talking and making, adjectives for people and for things, adverbs and connecting words, and more. Type the English meaning of each Japanese word, or type the Japanese word for each meaning — romaji turns into hiragana as you type and Space converts it to kanji. Each set keeps your best score, the words you miss can be reviewed together or added to your flashcards, and the whole list can be browsed on the Vocabulary page.
