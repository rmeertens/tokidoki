Studio Ghibli lines in Japanese: ジブリの名セリフ

From the films every family in Japan has watched a dozen times.

  • 見ろ、人がゴミのようだ！ — Look — the people are like garbage! Muska, Castle in the Sky. Said jokingly from any high viewpoint.
  • 40秒で支度しな！ — Get ready in 40 seconds! Dola the sky-pirate mum, Castle in the Sky. Every parent on a school morning.
  • 飛ばねえ豚は、ただの豚だ。 — A pig that doesn't fly is just a pig. Porco Rosso — about giving up on your dreams.
  • 黙れ小僧！ — Silence, boy! Moro the wolf goddess, Princess Mononoke.
  • 生きろ。 — Live. The one-word poster tagline of Princess Mononoke (1997).
  • トトロ、いたもん！ — Totoro was there, I tell you! Mei in My Neighbor Totoro, when no one believes her. 〜もん = a child's "because!".
  • まっくろくろすけ、出ておいで！ — Soot sprites, come out! My Neighbor Totoro. Children say it into dark corners.
  • 贅沢な名前だね。 — What an extravagant name. Yubaba in Spirited Away, just before she takes Chihiro's name away.
  • おちこんだりもしたけれど、私はげんきです。 — I've had my down days, but I'm doing fine. The poster line of Kiki's Delivery Service.
  • バ〜ルス！ — Balse! (the spell of destruction) Castle in the Sky. Every TV rerun, all of Japan tweets it at the same second.

Story: ハナの朝 (Hana's morning)

朝七時。お母さんの大きな声がします。 (Seven in the morning. Mum's loud voice rings out.) お母さん: 40秒で支度しな！ (Get ready in 40 seconds!) ハナ: まだ眠い… (I'm still sleepy…) ハナは暗い押し入れを開けました。 (Hana opened the dark closet.) ハナ: まっくろくろすけ、出ておいで！ (Soot sprites, come out!) 何もいませんでした。 (There was nothing there.) 学校で、ハナは友達に話しました。「森で大きいねこを見たよ！」 (At school, Hana told her friends: "I saw a giant cat in the forest!") 友達: うそだ！ (Liar!) ハナ: トトロ、いたもん！ (Totoro was there, I tell you!) 帰りに、お兄ちゃんが丘の上から町を見ました。 (On the way home, her big brother looked down at the town from a hill.) お兄ちゃん: 見ろ、人がゴミのようだ！ (Look — the people are like garbage!) ハナ: お兄ちゃん、それ、悪い人のセリフだよ。 (That's the bad guy's line, you know.)
