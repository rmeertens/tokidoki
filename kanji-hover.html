Kanji Hover Reference

A browsable version of the printable kanji answer-key sheets, covering JLPT N5 through N1. Each card shows the kanji and its English meaning stacked, one on top of the other, but only one side is visible at first — hover over a card to peek at the hidden side, or click it to reveal that side permanently. Switch the direction to show the kanji first and hide the meaning, or show the meaning first and hide the kanji, and filter which JLPT levels appear. Uses the same kanji lists as the Kanji Quiz and the printable practice sheets.
