Japanese text analyser: any text, broken down sentence by sentence

Paste a Japanese text — a news article, a message from a friend, song lyrics, a manga page or a passage from your textbook — and Claude splits it into sentences and explains each one the way Tokidoki's stories and news do: furigana on every kanji, the reading, dictionary form and meaning of every word, a natural English translation, and the grammar of each sentence (conjugations, particles, sentence patterns) explained for your JLPT level, N5 to N2. Tap a word to add it to your flashcards, or save a grammar point to review later. It uses your own Claude API key, which you add in your Tokidoki account.
