Easy Japanese news: 16 November – 22 November 2026

12 stories from Japan, each told in simple Japanese at JLPT N5, N4 and N3.

  • Japan posts a 476.5 billion yen trade deficit in October, the sixth in a row, even though exports hit a record high (10月の　貿易　6か月　続けて　赤字)
  • Japan lose 2–1 to Paraguay in Hajime Moriyasu's last match as coach; debutant Shio Fukuda scores Japan's goal (サッカー　森保監督の　最後の　試合)
  • A record 3.91 million foreign visitors come to Japan in October, the most for any month, even as visitors from China fall 47% (10月に　日本に　来た　外国の　人が　いちばん　多く)
  • Fukuoka mayoral election ends with no winner: none of a record 13 candidates gets a quarter of the vote, so the city will vote again (福岡市の　市長の　選挙を　もう　一度)
  • Princess Aiko arrives in Singapore for her second official overseas visit and meets President Tharman (愛子さまが　シンガポールへ)
  • Mercari and Suruga-ya to set up a joint company in the US to buy and sell Japanese anime, game and hobby goods (メルカリと　駿河屋が　アメリカで　新しい　会社)
  • Two suspected Russian military planes enter Japan's airspace near Okinoshima, Fukuoka; fighters scramble and Japan protests (ロシアの　飛行機が　日本の　空に)
  • RIKEN says someone broke into the Fugaku supercomputer's systems; data on up to 4,597 users may have been taken (コンピューター「富岳」に　だれかが　入りました)
  • Ami Nakai wins Skate America with a season-best 236.81 points, her second Grand Prix win in a row; Rino Matsuike third (中井亜美選手が　優勝)
  • Japan's economy shrinks slightly in July–September, its first contraction in a year, as household spending falls (日本の　経済が　少し　小さく　なりました)
  • Shuri Castle's main hall, destroyed by fire in 2019, is rebuilt; the interior is shown to the press ahead of public opening on November 23 (首里城の　新しい　建物が　できました)
  • Japan beat Brazil 3–2 in a friendly in Singapore with a stoppage-time goal by Tatsuhiro Sakamoto, their second win over Brazil (サッカー　日本が　ブラジルに　勝ちました)
