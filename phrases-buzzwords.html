Buzzwords of the year in Japanese: 流行語大賞

Winners of the yearly 新語・流行語大賞 — a snapshot of Japan, year by year.

  • いつやるか？今でしょ！ — When will you do it? Now, obviously! 2013: cram-school teacher Osamu Hayashi's TV ad line.
  • お・も・て・な・し — O-mo-te-na-shi (hospitality) 2013: Christel Takigawa's Tokyo 2020 Olympic bid speech, spelled out with a gesture.
  • じぇじぇじぇ！ — Jeh-jeh-jeh! (Whaaat?!) 2013: the surprised cry from Iwate dialect in the morning drama あまちゃん.
  • 倍返しだ！ — I'll pay you back double! 2013: banker Hanzawa Naoki's revenge line in the hit drama 半沢直樹.
  • 神ってる — Godlike (on a roll) 2016: the Hiroshima Carp manager about a player hitting walk-off homers.
  • インスタ映え — Looking good on Instagram 2017: food, cafés and places made for the photo.
  • そだねー — Yeah, that's right~ 2018: the women's curling team's Hokkaido-accent chatter during their bronze-medal Olympics.
  • ONE TEAM — One team 2019: the motto of Japan's Rugby World Cup team, which reached the quarter-finals at home.
  • 3密 — The Three Cs 2020: avoid closed spaces, crowds and close contact (密閉・密集・密接).
  • リアル二刀流 — Real two-sword style 2021: Shohei Ohtani pitching and hitting in the majors — like a samurai with two swords.
  • 村神様 — Lord Murakami 2022: Yakult Swallows slugger Munetaka Murakami, who hit 56 home runs.
  • アレ — "That" (A.R.E.) 2023: Hanshin Tigers manager Okada never said 優勝 (championship) — just "that". They won it.
  • ふてほど — Inappropriate (Futeho-do) 2024: short for the time-travel drama 不適切にもほどがある！"Way too inappropriate!".
  • ワイルドだろぉ？ — Pretty wild, huh? 2012: comedian Sugi-chan's catchphrase.
  • なでしこジャパン — Nadeshiko Japan 2011: the women's football team won the World Cup. なでしこ (a pink flower) = the ideal Japanese woman.

Story: おじいちゃんと流行語 (Grandpa's buzzwords)

おじいちゃんは流行語が大好きです。でも、ちょっと古いです。 (Grandpa loves buzzwords. But they're a little old.) ケン: 宿題、いつやろうかな。 (When should I do my homework…) おじいちゃん: いつやるか？今でしょ！ (When will you do it? Now, obviously!) ケン: 今日、ゲームで三回連続で勝ったよ！ (I won three games in a row today!) おじいちゃん: 神ってるね！ (You're on fire!) おばあちゃんがケーキの写真を撮っています。 (Grandma is taking a photo of a cake.) おじいちゃん: インスタ映えだね。 (Very Instagrammable.) ケン: おじいちゃん、すごいね。 (Grandpa, you're amazing.) おじいちゃん: ワイルドだろぉ？ (Pretty wild, huh?) おばあちゃん: そだねー。 (Yeah, that's right~)
