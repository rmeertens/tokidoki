Proverbs & four-kanji idioms in Japanese: ことわざ・四字熟語

Old wisdom you will hear in dramas, speeches and school.

  • 猿も木から落ちる。 — Even monkeys fall from trees. Even experts make mistakes.
  • 七転び八起き — Fall down seven times, get up eight. Never give up. The Daruma doll, which always rolls back upright, is its symbol.
  • 石の上にも三年 — Three years even on a stone. Sit on a cold stone long enough and it warms up: patience pays.
  • 猫に小判 — Gold coins to a cat. Wasted on someone who can't appreciate it. Like "pearls before swine".
  • 急がば回れ。 — If you're in a hurry, go the long way round. More haste, less speed.
  • 笑う門には福来る。 — Luck comes to the gate where people laugh. Keep smiling and good things follow. A New Year favourite.
  • 犬も歩けば棒に当たる。 — Even a dog that walks around bumps into a stick. Go out and something happens — good or bad.
  • 弘法も筆の誤り — Even Kobo Daishi slipped with his brush. Even the great calligrapher-monk made mistakes.
  • 一期一会 — One time, one meeting From the tea ceremony: treasure this moment — it will never come again.
  • 十人十色 — Ten people, ten colours Everyone is different.
  • 一石二鳥 — One stone, two birds The same idea as English, in four kanji.
  • 自業自得 — You reap what you sow. Your own actions brought it on — said with no sympathy at all.

Story: おばあちゃんのことわざ (Grandma's proverbs)

ケンのおばあちゃんは、いつもことわざで話します。 (Ken's grandma always talks in proverbs.) ケン: テスト、また悪かった… (I did badly on the test again…) おばあちゃん: 七転び八起きだよ。 (Fall down seven times, get up eight.) ケン: 今日、先生が漢字を間違えたんだ。 (The teacher wrote a kanji wrong today.) おばあちゃん: 猿も木から落ちる。 (Even monkeys fall from trees.) ケンは近道をして、道に迷いました。 (Ken took a shortcut and got lost.) おばあちゃん: 急がば回れ。 (If you're in a hurry, go the long way round.) お花見で、ケンは桜を見ないで、お団子ばかり食べました。 (At the cherry-blossom party, Ken ignored the blossoms and just ate dumplings.) おばあちゃん: 花より団子だね。 (Dumplings over flowers.) ケン: おばあちゃん、ことわざ多すぎるよ！ (Grandma, way too many proverbs!) おばあちゃん: 笑う門には福来る！ (Luck comes to the gate where people laugh!)
