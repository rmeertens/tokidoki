Video game messages in Japanese: ゲームの名セリフ

Text boxes from Dragon Quest, Pokémon and friends that became everyday jokes.

  • へんじがない。ただの しかばね のようだ。 — No reply. It seems to be just a corpse. Dragon Quest, talking to a skeleton. Said about a friend who won't answer messages.
  • ゆうべは おたのしみでしたね。 — You had fun last night, didn't you? The innkeeper in Dragon Quest I, after you stay the night with the princess.
  • ぼうけんのしょは きえてしまいました。 — Your adventure log has been erased. Dragon Quest's dreaded lost-save message — with a creepy jingle. A generation's trauma.
  • しかし まわりこまれてしまった！ — But you were cut off! Dragon Quest: you tried to run, and the monster blocked you. For any escape that fails.
  • いのちだいじに — Value your life. Dragon Quest's party tactic for "play it safe". The opposite: ガンガンいこうぜ "go all out!".
  • あ！ やせいの ピカチュウが とびだしてきた！ — Oh! A wild Pikachu jumped out! Pokémon's battle message. Swap in anything that suddenly appears: やせいの 部長が…
  • レベルが あがった！ — You levelled up! After any small win in real life.
  • 波動拳！ — Hadouken! (Surge fist) Ryu, Street Fighter. Yes, it's Japanese.
  • たたかう　にげる — Fight / Run The classic RPG battle menu. Meme: a picture of a hard choice with these two options.
  • コンティニュー？ — Continue? The arcade countdown — 10, 9, 8… Put in another coin!

Story: ケンのRPG (Ken's real-life RPG)

ケンの毎日は、ロールプレイングゲームです。 (Ken's everyday life is a role-playing game.) 朝、駅の角を曲がると… (In the morning, as he turns the corner at the station…) あ！やせいの 部長が とびだしてきた！ (Oh! A wild boss jumped out!) ケンは逃げようとしました。しかし まわりこまれてしまった！ (Ken tried to run. But he was cut off!) 部長: ケンくん、昨日の資料は？ (Ken, where's yesterday's report?) ケンは考えました。たたかう？にげる？ (Ken thought: fight? Run?) 夜、ケンは友達にメッセージを送りました。でも、返事がありません。 (That night, Ken messaged a friend. No reply.) へんじがない。ただの しかばね のようだ。 (No reply. It seems to be just a corpse.) 次の日、資料がやっと完成しました。 (The next day, the report was finally finished.) レベルが あがった！ (You levelled up!)
