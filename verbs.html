Japanese Verb Conjugation Practice — Genki I & II

Work through chapters 3–23 of Genki I and II. Each chapter introduces the conjugation forms taught in that unit — starting with the masu-form and masu-negative, then adding te-form, ta-form, nai-form, dictionary form, tai-form, potential, volitional, passive, causative, ba-conditional, and causative-passive. Both godan (u-verb) and ichidan (ru-verb) conjugation patterns are covered, along with the irregular verbs する and くる.

Cards are scheduled with spaced repetition (SRS) so you review what you need most, not what you already know. Free, no login required.
