Easy Japanese news: 2 November – 8 November 2026

18 stories from Japan, each told in simple Japanese at JLPT N5, N4 and N3.

  • Magnitude 5.7 earthquake in western Shizuoka shakes Aichi; 21 people hurt, no tsunami (愛知県で　大きな　地震が　ありました)
  • Government holds a nuclear disaster drill for an accident at the Tomari plant in Hokkaido (原発の　事故の　練習を　しました)
  • The "Godzilla Megamullion" seafloor formation found by the Japan Coast Guard is chosen as a geological heritage site (海の　底の　「ゴジラ」が　えらばれました)
  • Typhoon No. 32 forms near the Mariana Islands, the most typhoons in a year since 1994 (台風32号が　できました)
  • Magnitude 6.1 earthquake off the Kanto coast; strongest shaking of 3, no tsunami concern (関東の　海で　地震が　ありました)
  • BOJ Governor Ueda: reaching 2% is a milestone, but upside inflation risks remain (日銀の　総裁が　物の　ねだんの　話を　しました)
  • Lower House passes the bill cutting the consumption tax on food to 1%; it goes to the Upper House (食べ物の　消費税を　へらす　法案が　衆議院を　通りました)
  • Former JFA president Kozo Tashima says he will run for FIFA president (田嶋幸三さんが　FIFAの　会長に　なりたいと　言いました)
  • Government orders Chubu Electric to improve its operations over data fraud at the Hamaoka nuclear plant (国が　中部電力に　「よくしなさい」と　命令)
  • UNESCO evaluation body recommends Japanese calligraphy (shodo) for Intangible Cultural Heritage listing (「書道」が　ユネスコの　無形文化遺産に　なるかも)
  • NTT Docomo halts My docomo and its online shop over possible unauthorized access (ドコモの　サービスが　とまって　います)
  • US midterms: Democrats win control of Congress, US media report (アメリカの　中間選挙　民主党が　勝つ)
  • Typhoon No. 31 nears the Ogasawara Islands; heavy rain possible in Kanto and the Izu Islands through the 5th (台風31号で　あした　関東は　大雨かも)
  • Ruling parties propose a Nov. 6 Lower House committee vote on the bill cutting the consumption tax on food to 1% (食べ物の　消費税を　へらす　法律)
  • Tokyo starts a campaign letting people aged 29 and under use public baths for 300 yen (29歳までは　銭湯が　300円)
  • Japan launches its new Disaster Management Agency and asks people to stock up for at least three days (新しい　防災庁が　できました)
  • Government shows a rough figure of about 5,000 yen a year for an income-linked benefit for low earners (おかねが　すくない　人に　5000円ぐらい)
  • Nintendo’s Miyamoto Shigeru receives the Order of Culture, a first for the video game field (宮本さんが　文化勲章を　もらいます)
